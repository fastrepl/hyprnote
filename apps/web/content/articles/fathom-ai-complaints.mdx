Fathom is the notetaker people recommend when they want a generous free plan and a recap that arrives in half a minute. Unlimited recordings and transcripts, on a real free tier, is rare in this category. That is why the brand search is so clean.

The complaints are narrower. People who bounce off Fathom usually liked the recap. They ran into a bot that announces itself on a client call, a free plan that is unlimited until the AI you wanted caps at five calls, a bot-free desktop rollout limited by platform and account eligibility, or an auto-join that sometimes does not join at all.

**Disclosure:** I co-founded Anarlog, an open-source Fathom alternative. This post maps the frustrations that keep showing up in Google results, Reddit threads, vendor documentation, and Semrush traffic data. Our bias is toward local ownership and a choice of AI providers.

The organic keywords are branded and product-shaped: `fathom notetaker` at 6,600 monthly searches, `fathom note taker` at 2,900, `fathom` at 60,500 (a word that also means other things), `fathom video`, and `fathom login`. `fathom ai` has sat near 9,900 in earlier 2026 pulls. Brand searches go to Fathom. The surrounding SERP is "is Fathom free," "Fathom vs Otter," and alternatives pages.

Reddit and G2 are kinder to Fathom than to Otter. The praise is the free recording. The complaints are the bot, the advanced-summary allowance, and the days the bot does not show up.

## Language support splits by capture mode

Fathom's bot-free language support has expanded since the earlier English-only Mac rollout.

The [older language page lists 38 transcript languages](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/296192). The [October 2 upgrade guide](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/11577345) lists English plus 38 additional languages for native-language summaries and translated action items in Mac 3.0 from October 1. Availability varies by account; Windows is still listed as coming soon.

| Constraint | What Fathom does today |
| --- | --- |
| Bot capture languages | 38 transcript languages |
| Bot-free desktop | Mac 3.0, with multilingual availability rolling out by account |
| Windows bot-free | Not in that upgrade note yet |
| Android | No app |
| In-person | Weak. This is a video-call product |
| Offline | No |

If a meeting bot is the problem, check whether your account has the bot-free experience and whether its capture mode supports your platform. A supported language list also does not establish switching languages within one recording; test your actual conversation. ## The bot is the most common complaint

"Fathom Notetaker" in the participant list is the feature and the friction. On an internal standup, nobody cares. On a pitch, a board call, or a candidate interview, the join/leave announcement is the thing Reddit and sales forums call loud.

Fathom added a recording notice you can post to chat, and the 3.0 tray lets you pick Capture Off, Transcript Only, Audio Only, or Audio & Video with a bot. Those are the right controls. The default for most teams is still the visible bot, because that is how unlimited video recording works.

Failed auto-joins sit next to that. G2 reviewers describe the bot no-showing on the free plan, or dropping a long Zoom. There is no local fallback. If Fathom misses the call, the recap does not exist.

## Unlimited recording is not unlimited AI

Fathom's free plan includes unlimited recording, transcription, storage, and clips. Its [Free vs. Premium guide](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/5290881) limits advanced summaries to the first five calls each month. After that, the General/Enhanced summary remains available alongside the recording and transcript. Premium is $16 per user per month billed annually, or $20 billed monthly. Team and Business pricing climbs from there.

If the standard summary fits your work, call six does not require an upgrade. Check whether you need advanced templates or other paid AI features before treating the allowance as a reason to switch.

Training is the quieter setting. [Fathom says](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/296512) its AI subprocessors cannot train on customer data. Fathom itself may use de-identified meeting data to improve its models unless you opt out. Teams can set that org-wide. Individuals have to find the toggle. Fathom stores data in the United States.

## When these frustrations matter, and when they do not

Stay with Fathom if you want unlimited recording, its available capture mode and languages fit your calls, and standard summaries or a Premium seat match how you work.

Look elsewhere when the recurring complaints match your week:

- you need bot-free capture on Windows, or your Mac account does not yet have the required language rollout
- a Notetaker in the participant list is a client problem
- you need more than five advanced summaries without paying
- you meet on Android or in a room with no video call
- you need the record on your machine, or a non-US residency story
