A free AI notetaker can cover occasional meetings, but a free plan rarely includes every feature without limits. Transcription minutes, AI summaries, uploads, and storage can each have a different allowance. A tool that records unlimited meetings may still restrict how many it summarizes or how long it keeps them.

This guide compares nine ongoing free plans and the limits most likely to affect a meeting workflow. **Plan details checked October 14, 2026** against the linked provider pricing pages and help centers. This is a comparison of published terms, not a new hands-on accuracy test. Pricing screenshots below are historical illustrations; use the linked sources for current terms.

## How to Choose a Free Plan

Start with the meetings you actually need to capture. Four one-hour calls require 240 transcription minutes, so a 300-minute monthly plan can cover the total yet still cut off each call at a 30-minute limit. If you upload recordings instead of capturing live meetings, check the upload allowance separately.

Then check whether the AI allowance renews monthly or is a one-time allocation. For a lasting meeting archive, retention and export access matter as much as recording capacity. For sensitive conversations, review the capture method, processing destination, sharing defaults, and your organization's requirements before enabling a service.

If you only need transcripts, see our [free transcription software comparison](/blog/free-transcription-software/). For a broader selection, including paid products, use the [AI meeting assistant guide](/blog/best-ai-meeting-assistant-for-taking-notes/).

## Nine Free AI Notetakers and Their Upgrade Limits

### 1. Anarlog: Local Data and Your Choice of AI

[Anarlog](/) is an open-source meeting notetaker that captures audio without adding a bot and stores canonical sessions, notes, and transcripts in local SQLite. Markdown is an export format. It suits people who want local ownership and control over their AI stack.

![Anarlog](anarlog/blog/library/products/anarlog/desktop/2026-08-20-meeting-summary.jpg)

**Free allowance:** The [Free plan](/pricing/) includes on-device transcription, notes, templates, and your own AI-provider keys. Local transcription and local AI summaries are separate configurations; supported hardware, downloaded models, and available memory affect what can run on your machine. See the [offline transcription guide for Mac](/blog/offline-meeting-transcription-mac/) before choosing a setup.

**Upgrade trigger:** Pro adds hosted transcription, cloud AI, encrypted CloudSync, and other hosted features. Your own API keys can incur provider charges even while using the Free app. Hosted AI sends the relevant input to its provider; local storage alone does not make every configuration offline.

![Anarlog pricing](anarlog/blog/library/products/anarlog/web/2026-08-27-pricing.png)

### 2. tl;dv: Recording and Transcription

tl;dv is worth considering when recording and transcripts matter more than an unrestricted AI workflow.

![tl;dv](anarlog/blog/library/products/tldv/coaching/2026-08-27-speaker-insights.webp)

**Free allowance:** Its [help center](https://intercom.help/tldv/en/articles/8919450-what-are-the-limits-of-the-free-plan) lists unlimited recordings and transcripts, with recordings kept for three months. It describes full AI notes for the first ten meetings, then notes covering only the first ten minutes. It also lists lifetime allowances for AI prompts, reports, clips, and uploads.

**Important uncertainty:** tl;dv's [July 2026 comparison](https://tldv.io/blog/tldv-vs-jamie/) instead describes ten AI-noted meetings per month. These official sources disagree. Check your account's current allowance before relying on a renewal schedule.

**Upgrade trigger:** More extensive AI notes, cross-meeting analysis, or longer retention may justify a paid plan. Unlimited recording should not be read as unlimited permanent storage or unlimited full-meeting summaries.

![tldv pricing](anarlog/blog/articles/free-ai-notetakers/free-4.webp)

### 3. Fathom: Basic Summaries for Frequent Meetings

Fathom's ongoing free plan can support frequent calls when basic summaries are enough. Its [pricing page](https://www.fathom.ai/pricing) offers meeting-bot capture and a bot-free Mac beta; availability depends on the chosen method.

![Fathom](anarlog/blog/library/products/fathom/playlists/2026-08-27-highlight-playlists.webp)

**Free allowance:** The [Free versus Premium guide](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/5290881) lists unlimited recordings, storage, and transcripts. Five calls each month receive advanced summaries. After that, the General/Enhanced summary template remains available: the fifth advanced summary is not the end of free summaries.

**Upgrade trigger:** Premium expands advanced summaries and AI features. Team plans add shared workflows and controls. Choose an upgrade for features you need rather than assuming regular meeting attendance requires one.

![Fathom pricing](anarlog/blog/articles/free-ai-notetakers/free-6.webp)

### 4. Tactiq: A Browser-Based Transcript Workflow

Tactiq's Chrome extension provides live meeting transcripts without adding a meeting participant. It is a useful option for a modest number of browser-based calls.

![Tactiq ai](anarlog/blog/library/products/tactiq/google-meet/2026-08-27-live-transcript.webp)

**Free allowance:** The [current plan comparison](https://tactiq.io/buy) lists ten transcripts and five AI credits per month, plus transcript sharing and export. AI credits cover AI requests; they are a separate allowance from the number of transcribed meetings.

**Upgrade trigger:** Pro increases transcription access to unlimited transcripts but still lists ten AI credits per month. Team includes unlimited AI credits. Moving to unlimited transcripts therefore does not automatically remove the AI limit. Check your expected summary and question usage as well as your meeting count.

![Tactiq pricing](anarlog/blog/articles/free-ai-notetakers/free-8.webp)

### 5. Fireflies.ai: Cloud Transcripts with Conditional Free Access

Fireflies offers a cloud meeting workspace with several capture methods. Its free access has conditions that matter when deciding which meetings it may join.

![fireflies.ai](anarlog/blog/library/products/fireflies/contacts/2026-08-27-ai-bio.webp)

**Free allowance:** [Unlimited transcription requires Auto-join](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/8632872350-unlock-unlimited-transcriptions-on-the-fireflies-free-plan) for eligible meetings on newer Free accounts; manually inviting the bot alone does not unlock it. [Transcription credits](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1055386295-learn-about-transcription-credits) still apply to summaries and uploaded files. These are distinct from [AI credits for advanced features](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/2114151875-learn-about-ai-credits).

The [pricing page](https://fireflies.ai/pricing) lists limited summaries and 400 minutes of storage per team. Some help pages describe storage per seat, so check the workspace allowance before planning retention. AI-credit documentation also differs across pages; do not assume the listed 20 starting credits renew monthly.

**Upgrade trigger:** More storage, transcript/recording downloads, and expanded AI features. Review Auto-join and sharing settings before enabling unlimited transcripts. If those limits do not fit, compare [Fireflies alternatives](/blog/fireflies-ai-alternatives/).

![Fireflies pricing](anarlog/blog/articles/free-ai-notetakers/free-10.webp)

### 6. Otter.ai: Occasional Short Meetings

Otter's free Basic plan is easier to assess when you count both meeting duration and imported recordings.

![otter ai](anarlog/blog/library/products/otter/meeting-summary/2026-08-27-summary-card.webp)

**Free allowance:** The [pricing comparison](https://otter.ai/pricing) lists 300 transcription minutes per month, a 30-minute maximum per conversation, and three lifetime audio/video imports. The import allowance does not renew each month. Basic also includes live transcription, summaries, and AI Chat with its own usage limits.

**Upgrade trigger:** Pro provides 1,200 minutes, up to 90 minutes per meeting, and ten monthly imports. Business advertises unlimited in-app meetings and recordings, but imported-file minutes remain separately limited. A larger plan is not unlimited in every recording path. See [Otter alternatives](/blog/otter-ai-alternatives/) for other capture and storage options.

![Otter pricing](anarlog/blog/articles/free-ai-notetakers/free-12.webp)

### 7. MeetGeek: A Small Monthly Meeting Archive

MeetGeek combines capture, summaries, and meeting workflows. Its free plan can suit a small monthly meeting load, provided you manage retention.

![MeetGeek](anarlog/blog/library/products/meetgeek/meeting-insights/2026-08-27-meeting-analysis.webp)

**Free allowance:** The [Basic plan](https://meetgeek.ai/pricing) includes three transcription hours per month, AI summaries, three months of transcript storage, and one month of audio storage. Bot and bot-free capture options are listed, alongside file uploads.

**Upgrade trigger:** Pro increases transcription to 20 hours per month and extends transcript and audio retention. It also includes downloads and exports. Business offers broader transcription and storage access. Decide whether you need a durable archive before treating Basic as your only copy of a meeting.

![Meetgeek pricing](anarlog/blog/articles/free-ai-notetakers/free-14.webp)

### 8. Notta: Brief Recordings and Transcription Samples

Notta's free monthly total can look generous until you check the per-conversation limit. It is better suited to brief recordings or evaluating the service than complete long meetings.

![notta ai](anarlog/blog/library/products/notta/chrome-extension/2026-08-27-youtube-transcript.webp)

**Free allowance:** The [Free plan](https://www.notta.ai/en/pricing) lists 120 transcription minutes per month, but only three minutes per conversation. It also lists 50 file uploads and ten AI summaries per month. Upload count and monthly minutes do not remove the three-minute conversation limit.

**Upgrade trigger:** Pro provides 1,800 monthly minutes and a maximum of five hours per recording. This is a larger allowance, not unlimited recording duration. Check language, export, and translation requirements against the plan comparison before choosing a tier.

![Notta pricing](anarlog/blog/articles/free-ai-notetakers/free-16.webp)

### 9. Sonnet AI: A Small Bot-Free Note Allowance

Sonnet captures device audio without a meeting bot and emphasizes participant context, relationship tracking, and follow-up workflows.

![Sonnet meeting library with templates and categorized conversations](anarlog/blog/library/products/sonnet/meeting-library/2026-08-27-templates.webp)

**Free allowance:** Its [homepage](https://www.sonnetai.com/) advertises five free notes per month without a credit card. The public [pricing page](https://www.sonnetai.com/pricing) does not clearly expose current recording-duration or storage quotas. We cannot verify those limits from the available public information.

**Upgrade trigger:** More than five monthly notes or additional relationship-management features may require a paid plan. Check the in-app allowance and current upgrade terms before choosing it for long calls or a permanent archive; older pricing screenshots do not establish today's limits.

![Sonnet Free, Plus, Pro, and Enterprise pricing table](anarlog/blog/articles/free-ai-notetakers/free-18.webp)

## Which Free Notetaker Fits Your Meetings?

For frequent calls with basic summaries, Fathom's continuing free summary access is worth checking. For occasional short calls, compare Otter's per-meeting cap with MeetGeek's monthly hours and retention. For browser transcripts, count Tactiq's AI credits separately. Notta's three-minute free limit makes it a different proposition from a full-meeting tool.

If local ownership and a configurable AI stack are your priority, [download Anarlog](/download/) and choose the transcription and summary providers that fit your machine and workflow. Using local models requires setup and suitable hardware; using cloud providers requires reviewing their costs and data handling.

Whichever tool you choose, test one representative meeting, check the transcript and summary for errors, and confirm you can retrieve the notes in the format you need. An ongoing free plan is useful only when its actual limits fit your work.
