The best AI meeting summary tool for your team depends on what happens after the call. A short recap may be enough for a weekly sync. A customer interview needs supporting context; a sales call may need structured fields and a reviewed follow-up email.

This comparison focuses on how nine tools organize summaries, let you review the source, and move notes into your workflow. **Product details checked October 14, 2026** against the linked provider documentation. It is not a new accuracy benchmark or a ranking based on hands-on testing of every product.

A transcript and a summary solve different problems. Keep the transcript as supporting evidence, then check whether the summary preserves decisions, unresolved questions, and the next steps people actually agreed to.

## What Makes a Useful Meeting Summary?

Look for three things in the output: decisions with their context, actions with confirmed owners and dates, and questions that remain open. A polished paragraph can still be wrong about any of them.

Templates help you request a consistent structure. They do not establish that the content is correct. Source links, timestamps, or a nearby transcript make review easier, but they are not a guarantee against invented or misattributed details.

Check the full workflow as well. Can you correct the note before sharing it? Can you find the supporting conversation? Can you keep or export the result? Who receives the audio, transcript, summary, and recap email? Bot-free recording describes capture, not where AI processing happens.

## Nine Tools for Different Summary Workflows

### 1. Anarlog: Local Meeting Data and a Configurable Summary

Anarlog is an open-source AI meeting notetaker with canonical sessions, notes, and transcripts stored locally in SQLite. Markdown is an export format. It combines your own meeting notes with the transcript and other meeting context to generate a summary.

**Disclosure:** I'm a co-founder of Anarlog.

![Current Anarlog meeting summary with recent meetings in the sidebar](anarlog/blog/library/products/anarlog/desktop/2026-08-20-meeting-summary.jpg)

The [summary settings](https://docs.anarlog.so/customize-summaries) offer named templates and a customizable Auto format. For example, request decisions first, then actions with owners and due dates. Changing a template does not rewrite an existing summary; regenerate it to apply the new format.

![Anarlog Settings → Intelligence listing the AI providers you can configure](anarlog/blog/library/products/anarlog/desktop/2026-09-23-settings-intelligence-providers.png)

[Transcription and Intelligence are independent selections](https://docs.anarlog.so/models-and-providers). A local speech model does not make summaries local. Choose a local Intelligence endpoint for local summaries; hosted selections send the required text and context to a provider. Model downloads, hardware, and provider costs still matter.

Use the separate memo, summary, and transcript views to [review and edit the result](https://docs.anarlog.so/notes). The [documented offline Mac test](/blog/offline-meeting-transcription-mac/) completed but produced an incorrect owner and deadline. Treat output as a draft, and measure latency on your own setup rather than expecting instant, error-free notes. See [current plans](/pricing/) for Free and hosted Pro boundaries.

### 2. Read AI: Meeting Reports and Connected Search

Read AI fits a workflow where meeting notes are one part of a broader search across meetings, email, and messages.

![Read AI meeting summary with generated summary, action items, and transcript context](anarlog/blog/library/products/read-ai/meeting-summary/2026-08-30-instant-summary.png)

Its [plan comparison](https://www.read.ai/plans-pricing) describes meeting reports with summaries, topics, action items, and key questions, plus Ask Read for connected search. This can help when the next question depends on several conversations rather than one recap.

Free includes five meeting transcripts per month. Playback and workspace controls vary by plan, so check those separately from summary access. Before connecting additional sources, review what can be searched and how reports are distributed. Engagement metrics are separate from the factual record of decisions; verify the recap against its source.

### 3. Fathom: Basic Recaps and Advanced Templates

Fathom is a candidate for frequent meetings when a basic recap is enough, with advanced templates available for more specialized notes.

![Fathom Recap workspace with meeting video, AI summary, and action items](anarlog/blog/library/products/fathom/meeting-recap/2026-08-30-recap.webp)

Its [Free versus Premium guide](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/5290881) distinguishes five advanced summaries per month from continuing General/Enhanced summaries. Free summaries do not stop after the fifth call. Premium expands advanced summaries and AI features.

The [pricing page](https://www.fathom.ai/pricing) also lists meeting-bot capture and a bot-free Mac beta. Choose the capture method that fits your calls, then evaluate the summary template and review workflow. Try the chosen template on a representative call and check decisions against the transcript.

### 4. Jamie: Topic-Based Notes and Editable Tasks

Jamie can suit people who want bot-free capture followed by topic-based notes and a separate task list.

![Jamie meeting note with a structured summary and Ask AI controls](anarlog/blog/articles/ai-meeting-summary-tools/summary-5.webp)

The [Meeting Output guide](https://docs.meetjamie.ai/getting-started/summary) describes an executive summary, a fuller topic-based summary, a transcript, tasks, and a private scratchpad. You can edit the output and use templates or change the detail level.

Bot-free does not mean on-device transcription. Jamie's [data-handling documentation](https://docs.meetjamie.ai/faqs-troubleshooting/data) says audio is uploaded for server processing, then deleted after transcription; the transcript is processed through an AI API to generate notes.

Its [Free plan](https://www.meetjamie.ai/pricing) lists ten AI notes per month with a 30-minute meeting limit. Check longer-call allowances before adopting it for hour-long interviews. Recording without a bot is a different decision from approving its cloud data path.

### 5. Fellow: Shared Meeting Structure and Follow-Up

Fellow combines meeting preparation and shared notes with generated summaries and action items.

![Fellow Ask AI panel beside shared talking points and assigned action items](anarlog/blog/library/products/fellow/ask-ai/2026-08-27-talking-points.webp)

Its [meeting-notes documentation](https://fellow.ai/features/ai-meeting-notes) describes bot and bot-free desktop recording, custom note templates, and Ask Fellow for questions across meetings. It also connects notes and tasks to project-management and CRM tools. This makes the agenda-to-follow-up workflow worth evaluating alongside the recap itself.

The [current Free plan](https://fellow.ai/pricing) lists five lifetime AI notes and recordings per user, not ten renewing monthly. Advanced recording permissions and transcript redaction appear on higher tiers. Verify which controls your plan includes rather than assuming every account has the same organization-level safeguards.

### 6. Fireflies.ai: Searchable Recaps and AI Workflows

Fireflies brings summaries, searchable transcripts, and AI features into a cloud meeting workspace.

![Fireflies meeting workspace with AI summary, transcript, speakers, and playback controls](anarlog/blog/library/products/fireflies/meeting-summary/2026-08-27-meeting-workspace.webp)

Its [pricing page](https://fireflies.ai/pricing) separates limited Free summaries from expanded paid summary access, downloads, storage, and conversation intelligence. Unlimited eligible transcription is not the same as unlimited summaries or advanced AI requests.

[AI credits](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/2114151875-learn-about-ai-credits) cover advanced features such as custom summary sections and AskFred in Live Assist. Other [transcription credits](https://guide.fireflies.ai/articles/1055386295-learn-about-transcription-credits) govern Free summaries and uploaded files. Check the actual workspace allowances; official documents differ on some credit and storage terms.

Review Auto-join, recap recipients, and integration settings before enabling automatic capture or distribution. A useful summary workflow should send the corrected note to the intended audience.

### 7. Otter.ai: Summaries Beside a Live Transcript

Otter provides a shared transcript-oriented workflow with generated summaries and AI Chat for follow-up questions.

![Otter product-launch meeting summary with transcript and meeting details](anarlog/blog/articles/ai-meeting-summary-tools/summary-8.webp)

Its [plan comparison](https://otter.ai/pricing) lists automated summaries with action items and outlines, live transcription, collaboration, and separate AI Chat limits. These features can help teams revisit a conversation, but the generated actions still need confirmation.

Basic includes 300 monthly transcription minutes and a 30-minute conversation cap. Imported-file allowances are separate. Check duration and import limits before treating a short-call trial as evidence that the same plan covers long meetings. Its current transcription languages include German, Japanese, and Chinese alongside English, Spanish, and French. Check the supported languages and interface requirements for your meetings.

### 8. Avoma: Structured Sales Notes and CRM Handoff

Avoma is a candidate when a meeting recap needs to feed a sales or customer-success workflow.

![Avoma meeting workspace with video, transcript, and AI-generated chapters](anarlog/blog/library/products/avoma/meeting-notes/2026-08-30-auto-chapters.webp)

Its [meeting-assistant page](https://www.avoma.com/ai-meeting-assistant) describes customizable notes, topic chapters, follow-up email drafts, and CRM updates. Review the extracted fields and commitments before letting a recap become a customer email or deal record.

The [pricing comparison](https://www.avoma.com/pricing) separates meeting-assistant features from revenue add-ons. It lists a 14-day trial and bills paid recorder seats; free viewer access is not ongoing free recording. The advertised $19 recorder-seat price uses annual billing. Check the monthly billing basis and template/add-on access rather than comparing that figure with another tool's monthly subscription.

### 9. MeetGeek: Templates and Recap Distribution

MeetGeek is worth evaluating when different meeting types need different summary structures and distribution rules.

![MeetGeek meeting analysis with video, transcript, speaker metrics, and AI insights](anarlog/blog/library/products/meetgeek/meeting-insights/2026-08-27-meeting-analysis.webp)

Its [summary customization guide](https://support.meetgeek.ai/en/articles/10493568-meeting-summary-customization) explains editable summaries and section prompts that control what the recap captures. The [summary email guide](https://support.meetgeek.ai/en/articles/8028156-ai-meeting-minutes-your-summary-email) describes recap recipients and paid-plan sharing controls. Check those settings before relying on an opportunity to edit a note before everyone receives it.

The [Basic plan](https://meetgeek.ai/pricing) includes three transcription hours per month, with separate transcript/audio retention limits. Capture method, templates, and archive access should each fit the workflow you intend to use.

## Test the Summary You Will Actually Share

Use one representative meeting and write down the expected decisions and commitments before comparing the output. Include a rejected proposal, a task with no assigned owner, and a date that differs from an earlier suggestion. A useful recap should preserve those distinctions instead of turning every discussion into a commitment.

Review the same details for each tool:

- **Decisions:** Does it distinguish an agreement from an idea that was rejected or left open?
- **Actions:** Are names, dates, amounts, and task owners supported by the conversation?
- **Source review:** Can you find the relevant transcript or recording without rereading the whole meeting?
- **Editing and sharing:** Can you correct errors before sending the note, and confirm who receives it?
- **Practical cost:** Does the plan cover the meeting length, summary template, retention, and exports you need?

Measure processing time from the end of your own recording to a usable, reviewed note. A vendor's speed claim and a short demo do not establish performance on your audio, language, or model.

## Choose Around the Handoff

For local meeting ownership and control over the AI provider, evaluate Anarlog's templates and model configuration. For a connected knowledge workspace, evaluate search and sharing. For revenue teams, examine CRM field mapping and follow-up review. For regular internal meetings, shared agendas and consistent task ownership may matter more than a long feature list.
