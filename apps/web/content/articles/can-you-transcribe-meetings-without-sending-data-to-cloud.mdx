**Yes, but local transcription is only one part of a cloud-free meeting workflow.** Audio capture, speech recognition, summaries, storage, and connected features can follow different data paths. Check each one before assuming that a local transcript means the entire meeting stays on your device.

Anarlog stores its canonical meeting record in local SQLite. You can keep transcription and summaries local too, with a supported setup. Choosing a hosted model or another connected feature changes that boundary.

## Local storage and local processing are different

![Cloud transcription relies on vendor servers, while on-device transcription keeps processing on your Mac](anarlog/blog/articles/can-you-transcribe-meetings-without-sending-data-to-cloud/cloud-vs-local-transcription.png)

A cloud transcription service receives audio to turn speech into text. Saving its resulting transcript locally does not undo that upload. Likewise, a local speech model can create a transcript that a cloud language model later receives to generate a summary.

Anarlog keeps those choices separate in **Settings → Transcription** and **Settings → Intelligence**. Changing one does not change the other. The [models and providers documentation](https://docs.anarlog.so/models-and-providers) explains their inputs and requirements.

| Setup | Audio processing | Summary processing | What leaves the device for AI processing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| On-device transcription and local Intelligence | Local | Local | Meeting content does not need a hosted AI provider |
| On-device transcription and hosted Intelligence | Local | Hosted | Text and context needed for the summary request |
| Hosted transcription and local Intelligence | Hosted | Local | Audio needed for transcription |
| Hosted transcription and hosted Intelligence | Hosted | Hosted | Audio for transcription, then text and context for summaries |

Using your own API key gives you a choice of provider; it does not make that provider local. Retention and training policies depend on the service and agreement you use.

## Check your hardware and language first

Anarlog's built-in on-device transcription options require a supported Apple Silicon Mac. Apple Speech has additional macOS 26 and system-language requirements. An Intel Mac, Windows installer, or Linux installer does not establish that these built-in models are available there.

Language coverage also depends on the selected speech model. The app's overall language support is not a promise that every local model handles the same languages. Check the current [on-device model requirements](https://docs.anarlog.so/models-and-providers#on-device-models) before downloading one.

Local and cloud accuracy or speed depend on the model, hardware, language, and recording. A cloud service is not automatically the faster or more accurate choice. Try a representative sample before relying on either setup.

## Prepare both AI stages before disconnecting

Choose and download your speech model, then configure a local Intelligence provider and download its language model. A local server must remain available to Anarlog during summaries and chat.

For the actual setup and a short disconnected test, follow the [offline meeting transcription guide](/blog/offline-meeting-transcription-mac/). It covers audio permissions, model preparation, and checking the transcript and summary separately. The [official offline checklist](https://docs.anarlog.so/offline) explains what can work without a network.

## Check features beyond the models

Local AI selections do not prevent other features from sending data elsewhere:

- CloudSync can replicate encrypted notes to your other devices.
- Sharing uploads the generated summary and any audio you explicitly include.
- Cloud API & Connectors uploads a separate server-readable copy when enabled.
- Chat's web search sends the search query to a hosted endpoint, even with local models.

Avoid those features for a meeting that must remain on one device. Review usage analytics and error-reporting preferences separately; they are different from meeting-content processing. The [data and privacy guide](https://docs.anarlog.so/data-and-privacy) describes these boundaries.

## Can you transcribe without retaining the audio?

Transcription and permanent audio retention are separate choices. Anarlog offers **Don't save** under **Settings → Meetings → Audio file retention**, but capture still uses temporary audio for recovery. Once that audio is deleted, an incomplete transcript cannot be repaired from it. Choose retention deliberately rather than treating transcription as a guarantee that no audio file ever existed.

If you are comparing broader deployment choices, the [local AI meeting notes comparison](/blog/local-ai-meeting-notes/) separates desktop local processing from hosted services. A configurable desktop app is not proof of a complete self-hosted storage service or automatic compliance.
