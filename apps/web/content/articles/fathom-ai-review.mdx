Fathom is the AI notetaker people recommend when they are tired of Otter's minute counter. The recordings are unlimited. The catch is that the useful AI has its own meter.

**Disclosure:** I co-founded Anarlog, an open-source Fathom alternative. This review is based on Fathom's product, pricing, help center, and public usage data. Our bias is toward local ownership and a choice of AI providers.

The pitch is simple. Record Zoom, Google Meet, and Teams. Get a recap. Clip the moments. Do it on a free plan that does not cap recordings. Semrush put [fathom.video at about 656,000 visits](https://www.semrush.com/website/fathom.video/overview/) in July 2026. That is smaller than Otter or Fireflies and large enough that the product is not a secret.

We already keep a running list of [Fathom alternatives](/blog/fathom-ai-alternatives) and a separate writeup of [why people complain about Fathom](/blog/fathom-ai-complaints). This is the first-party review: what the product is good at, where the free plan actually stops, and why Fathom 3.0 is not a drop-in replacement for the bot.

## What Fathom is

Fathom is a meeting recorder with an opinionated recap. It joins as a bot (or, on the 3.0 beta, records from the Mac). After the call you get a structured summary, action items on paid plans, and a player you can clip.

Fathom 3.0 also adds a private scratchpad and live summary. Typed notes can feed into the final summary, so the older distinction between Fathom's recorder and a notepad is less clear. Compare the actual editing and capture workflow available to your account.

## What it does well

### The recap is the product

Fathom's shareable recap is the reason the brand exists. Clean enough to paste into Slack. Fast enough that people actually send it. The in-app recap is the artifact, not a transcript dump.

On the free plan you get instant summaries. Fathom's [Free vs. Premium guide](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/5290881) gives advanced summaries for the first five calls each month, then keeps the General/Enhanced template available. Premium removes the advanced-summary allowance.

### Unlimited recordings on free

This is the Otter contrast. Otter's free plan is a minute budget. Fathom's free plan is unlimited recordings and transcription. You can live here for a long time if instant summaries and the player are enough.

You cannot live here if you need Ask Fathom, CRM field sync, coaching, or more than a handful of advanced summaries.

### Clips and the player

Jump-to-moment and clip sharing are solid. For teams that circulate "the 90 seconds where we decided X," Fathom is closer to tl;dv than to Granola. Granola barely wants to be a player. Fathom is one.

### Price, if you do pay

[Fathom's pricing page](https://www.fathom.video/pricing) (August 2026):

| Plan | Monthly | Annual equivalent | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | $0 | $0 | Unlimited recordings and transcription. Instant summaries. Bot-free beta (Mac). Advanced summaries limited. |
| **Premium** | $20/mo | $16/mo billed annual | Advanced summaries, action items, Ask Fathom. |
| **Team** | $19/user/mo | $15/user/mo annual | Shared workspace. Two-user minimum. |
| **Business** | $34/user/mo | $25/user/mo annual | CRM field sync, coaching. |
| **Enterprise** | Talk to sales | Talk to sales | HIPAA with a signed BAA is listed here. |

Annual exists. Granola's Business plan does not do that. If you are comparing those two, the billing shape is part of the comparison.

## Where it falls short

### The bot is still the default

Fathom 3.0 is the Mac bot-free experience. Its [October 2 upgrade guide](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/11577345) describes a multilingual rollout that varies by account. The earlier English-only restriction is outdated; Windows is still listed as coming soon.

The bot is competent. It is also the thing privacy-sensitive customers reject, and the thing that fails to join when waiting rooms and auth get in the way. If "no bot" is why you opened Fathom, read the 3.0 constraints before you switch the team off Otter.

### Advanced summaries are the real free-plan wall

Advanced summaries have a monthly allowance; standard summaries continue after it runs out. Five advanced summaries may be enough if you rarely need a specialized template. Teams that need those templates on every call should compare the paid plan.

Premium at $20/month is in Otter Pro territory. Fair if you use Ask Fathom and action items. Not a spiritual successor to "Fathom is free."

### The 3.0 scratchpad contributes to summaries

The upgrade guide describes private notes during the call and highlights where those notes were incorporated into the summary. Fathom can use what you type; it is inaccurate to describe 3.0 as only an automatic recap tool. Test its editor alongside [Granola](/blog/granola-ai-complaints/) or Anarlog if writing during a meeting is central to your workflow.

### Language availability depends on the rollout

The [older language page lists 38 transcript languages](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/296192). The newer upgrade guide lists English plus 38 languages for native-language summaries and translated action items in Mac 3.0 from October 1. Confirm your account's eligibility and test mixed-language recording separately. See our [multilingual comparison](/blog/best-multilingual-ai-notetaker/).

### HIPAA is Enterprise

The pricing page lists HIPAA with a signed BAA on Enterprise. Do not put PHI on Free or Premium because the recap is convenient. [HIPAA-compliant AI notetakers](/blog/hipaa-compliant-ai-notetaker) is the longer version.

## Who Fathom is for

**Yes, if:**

- You want a free recap tool for Zoom / Meet / Teams and you can live with a bot.
- Clips matter more than a live notepad.
- The language support enabled for your capture mode covers the team.
- You might pay Premium for Ask Fathom, not because you hit a recording cap.

**No, if:**

- You need bot-free on Windows, or your Mac account lacks the language features you need.
- You need a local note store or a choice of AI providers.
- You need self-serve HIPAA.
- You need the language story in [the multilingual post](/blog/best-multilingual-ai-notetaker).
