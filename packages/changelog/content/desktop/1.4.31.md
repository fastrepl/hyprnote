## Recording

- Keep recording a call when another app briefly uses and releases the microphone.
- Delay a scheduled recording while the previous call still holds the microphone, so its remaining audio stays out of the next meeting's note.
- Keep local transcription running through long silent stretches instead of repeatedly restarting the recording stream.
- Preserve fresh speaker groups when re-transcribing a meeting, and retain your speaker label when the original microphone channel can be identified reliably.
