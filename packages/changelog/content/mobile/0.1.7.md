## iOS TestFlight

- View recent sync activity in Settings, including retries, pending changes, and the last successful sync in the current app session.
- See clearer connection and timeout errors when encrypted sync cannot reach the server, without exposing private request URLs.
- Recognize when sync is connecting, working, or complete with clearer status labels.

The activity log covers the current app session and clears when you restart the app or switch accounts. This beta adds diagnostics for investigating sync failures; it does not establish that the reported retry issue is resolved.
