Tactiq alternatives make sense when you need a different meeting archive, local AI processing, video clips, or team follow-up workflow. Leaving a browser extension is no longer the only reason to switch: Tactiq now has a Mac app as well.

**Sources checked October 15, 2026.** This guide compares current published workflows and plan boundaries. It does not report a new seven-product speed or accuracy benchmark.

## Check Tactiq's desktop option before switching

Tactiq's [current Mac installation guide](https://help.tactiq.io/en/articles/17447226-how-to-install-the-tactiq-mac-app-for-zoom-slack-and-microsoft-teams) documents capture for Zoom desktop calls, Slack desktop calls/huddles, and Microsoft Teams desktop meetings. It uses macOS audio capture without joining as a participant and uploads the meeting to your Tactiq dashboard afterward. Setup requires microphone and screen/system-audio permissions.

The browser extension remains the documented path for Google Meet and browser/PWA Teams meetings. The Mac app does not cover browser Slack huddles, iPhone, or iPad; the help guide describes Windows support as being explored.

Tactiq's [public download page](https://tactiq.io/download) still labels the Mac app beta and describes Zoom-only support. The installation guide has broader coverage, so follow the current account setup instructions and test your exact client before assuming it works. A supported Mac desktop call may no longer justify changing tools by itself.

If plan limits are the problem, compare transcript allowances and AI credits separately in the [free AI notetaker guide](/blog/free-ai-notetakers/). If you need a different capture or storage model, start below.

## Seven Tactiq alternatives by workflow

| Tool | Capture choice | Reason to consider it | Check before switching |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Anarlog | Desktop microphone/system audio without a bot | Local meeting records and independently selected AI providers | Local speech and summary setup, hardware, exports |
| Otter.ai | Meeting Notetaker or Mac/Windows device capture | Shared searchable archive and attendance when you are absent | Capture mode, minutes, import/export limits |
| Granola | Device audio alongside your own notes | A shared workspace built around expanding personal notes | History, sharing, export scope |
| Fathom | Meeting bot or Mac bot-free beta | Recordings, clips, summaries and CRM follow-up | Beta availability, advanced summaries, team plan |
| Fireflies.ai | Meeting bot, desktop/mobile and browser options | Integrations, search and conversation analytics | Storage, AI credits, export and analytics tier |

| Notta | App, browser, uploads and Desktop Privacy Mode | Multiple capture paths with a distinct local transcription mode | Mode-specific processing, languages and limits |

Bot-free capture does not establish local processing or participant permission. Review the selected recording method, AI provider, retention, and recipients independently. The [bot-free comparison](/blog/bot-free-ai-meeting-assistants/) explains those distinctions in more detail.

## 1. Anarlog: local meeting records and your choice of AI

![Current Anarlog meeting summary view](anarlog/blog/library/products/anarlog/desktop/2026-08-20-meeting-summary.jpg "char-editor-width=80")

**Disclosure:** I'm a co-founder of Anarlog.

[Anarlog](/) is an open-source AI meeting notetaker. It captures microphone and system audio without adding a meeting participant, while you can write your own notes beside the transcript. Canonical sessions, notes, and transcripts are stored in local SQLite; recordings are separate files, and Markdown is an export format.

The reason to choose it over Tactiq is control over the meeting record and processing setup. [Transcription and Intelligence are independent settings](https://docs.anarlog.so/models-and-providers): choosing local speech recognition does not make a hosted summary local. Hosted transcription receives audio; hosted Intelligence receives the text/context for its request. BYOK uses your selected provider and can incur provider charges.

The [Free plan](/pricing/) supports local transcription on supported hardware and BYOK; Pro adds hosted AI and other connected features. For a local configuration, use the [offline Mac setup](/blog/offline-meeting-transcription-mac/) and verify both speech and summary models. Check current platform availability before a team rollout, and test the export format you need rather than assuming it matches Tactiq's workspace.

## 2. Otter.ai: an archive with bot and device-capture choices

![Otter Notetaker calendar controls, visible meeting participant, and live transcript workspace](anarlog/blog/library/products/otter/notetaker/2026-07-17-notetaker-overview.png)

*Otter's official Notetaker overview shows its calendar, visible recording participant, and transcript workspace. Source: [Otter](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/4425393298327-Otter-Notetaker-Overview).*

[Otter's desktop app](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/35973988280215-Otter-Desktop-App-Mac-Windows) supports botless recording on Mac and Windows. Its calendar-connected Notetaker is a separate choice that can join supported meetings as a visible participant. Compare the mode you will actually use, especially if you want attendance when you are away from the call.

Its [plan comparison](https://otter.ai/pricing) lists shared meeting workflows, AI Chat within/across meetings, and search/export features. Basic includes 300 monthly transcription minutes and three lifetime file imports; higher tiers change duration, import, collaboration, and export allowances. A monthly minutes allowance is not the same as an unlimited archive-import tool.

Choose it when a hosted searchable archive fits your team. Review access, retention, capture notifications, and export requirements separately from whether a bot appears. This guide does not rank Otter's search accuracy against other products.

## 3. Granola: notes you write, then expand and share

![Granola vs tactiq](anarlog/blog/library/products/granola/notepad/2026-08-27-meeting-summary.webp "char-editor-width=80")

[Granola](https://www.granola.ai/pricing) combines device-audio capture with the notes you type during a meeting. Its published plans include AI-enhanced notes, chat, templates, and shared folders. It is a candidate when you want personal notes and team context in one hosted workspace rather than a standalone transcript export.

Basic includes 30 days of meeting history; Business adds unlimited notes/history and wider integrations. Check your account's sharing and model-training settings. Device capture does not establish that the transcription or summary runs locally.

Granola's [historical CSV export](https://docs.granola.ai/help-center/sharing/exporting-notes) includes owned summarized notes and transcripts from selected workspaces, including eligible older notes outside Basic's visible history. Deleted notes and notes without summaries are excluded. Use the supported export rather than depending on direct access to an internal database. The [Granola switching guide](/blog/granola-ai-alternatives/) covers that workflow.

## 4. Fathom: meeting recordings and reviewed follow-up

![Fathom vs tactiq](anarlog/blog/articles/tactiq-alternatives/image-14.png "char-editor-width=80")

[Fathom's current pricing page](https://www.fathom.ai/pricing) lists unlimited individual recordings and transcriptions, plus a choice of bot capture or **bot-free capture in beta for Mac**. Confirm the beta option in your account before treating it as available for every platform or meeting.

Recordings, clips, playlists, summaries, and CRM follow-up make it useful when teammates need a moment from a call as well as written notes. The [Free/Premium help comparison](https://help.fathom.video/en/articles/5290881) distinguishes five advanced summaries each month from the General/Enhanced summary that remains available afterward. Free recording capacity does not mean every advanced AI feature is unlimited.

Current Team and Business plans have a two-user minimum and different collaboration, CRM, and coaching features. Check the billing period and required tier on the pricing page. Review generated summaries and CRM recipients; no comparative speed or accuracy test is claimed here.

## 5. Fireflies.ai: integrations and conversation analytics

![Fireflies vs tactiq](anarlog/blog/articles/tactiq-alternatives/image-15.png "char-editor-width=80")

[Fireflies' plan comparison](https://fireflies.ai/pricing) lists meeting search, desktop/mobile apps, a browser recording option without its bot, integrations, and higher-tier conversation analytics. It is worth evaluating when the destination for the notes matters: a CRM, task workflow, or team archive.

The Free plan currently lists **400 minutes of storage per team**, limited AI summaries, and 20 AI credits. Storage capacity is not a monthly transcription allowance. Pro adds transcript/summary/recording downloads and broader integrations; Business adds conversation intelligence and team analytics. Check credit and rate limits alongside transcription capacity.

Choose the recording method explicitly and inspect automatic sharing before turning on a team workflow. A desktop app or browser extension does not make a hosted archive local. Confirm export availability if you expect to leave with recordings and transcripts later.

## 6. tl;dv: share clips and revisit a call library

![tl;dv vs tactiq](anarlog/blog/articles/tactiq-alternatives/image-16.png "char-editor-width=80")

[tl;dv](https://tldv.io/) centers its workflow on meeting recordings, clips, team knowledge, custom notes, and insights across conversations. It also advertises bot-free capture, so do not assume a visible bot is required for every current mode.

It can suit a team that sends short excerpts to colleagues or uses a shared call library for follow-up. Inspect the exact supported meeting client and capture setup, then check who can open a clip or library link.

Recording, AI-note allowances, uploads, and retention are separate plan conditions. Review the [product site](https://tldv.io/) before treating a free recording offer as unlimited AI analysis or permanent storage. Current exact quotas and paid prices were not consistently available during this source check, so they are not quoted here. For summary review criteria, see the [meeting summary guide](/blog/ai-meeting-summary-tools/).

## 7. Notta: distinguish cloud capture from local transcription

![Notta vs tactiq](anarlog/blog/library/products/notta/chrome-extension/2026-08-27-youtube-transcript.webp "char-editor-width=80")

[Notta](https://www.notta.ai/en/pricing) offers app/browser recording, file imports, meeting workflows, transcription, and translation options. Compare the particular language, client, and processing mode you need rather than choosing from a headline language count.

Its [Desktop Privacy Mode](https://www.notta.ai/en/features/privacy-mode) records and transcribes locally with related files kept on the device. Initial setup requires an offline model download, and language coverage depends on that model. Other Notta capture modes use cloud workflows; choosing the app does not automatically enable local processing or local AI summaries.

Privacy Mode's Pro local transcription does not consume cloud transcription minutes, though device resources still matter for long recordings. If you need cloud translation or AI summaries, check those plan allowances separately. Test speaker labels, technical terms, and export formats with your own meeting before adopting it for an international team.

## Preserve your Tactiq transcripts before moving

Tactiq's [download guide](https://help.tactiq.io/en/articles/9784666-how-to-download-your-tactiq-meeting-transcript) documents PDF and TXT export **one transcript at a time**. Open the transcript and choose Export to PDF or Export to TXT. Both include the meeting name/date/duration, speaker names, highlights, and full transcript; the PDF also links back to the original Tactiq page.

1. Export the meetings you need and open the files to confirm they are complete.
2. Keep the original exports in your own archive before changing subscriptions or deleting records.
3. Check the new tool's supported import format with one meeting. An available export is not proof of an automatic migration into another service.
4. Test your exact meeting client, permissions, languages, and summary workflow before moving the rest of your team.

Exported transcript files do not recreate source audio that was never retained. Check your existing capture mode if you need recordings for later review.

## Choose the change you actually need

Stay with Tactiq if its browser or supported Mac workflow already captures your meetings and its transcript/AI allowances fit. Consider Anarlog for a local canonical record and separately configured AI; Granola for notes-first collaboration; Otter for its archive and attendance workflows; Fathom for recordings and CRM follow-up; Fireflies for integrations and analytics; tl;dv for shared clips; or Notta for its mode-specific capture and transcription options.

Run the same consented sample meeting through your preferred setup, then review names, decisions, action owners, and deadlines. Compare the export and sharing result as well as the transcript. That gives you a useful basis for choosing without relying on unsupported rankings or promises of error-free output.
