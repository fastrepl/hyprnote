Otter AI is worth considering if you want a shared, searchable meeting workspace and are comfortable with hosted processing. Its recording options are broader than a calendar bot: Otter also has desktop capture and mobile recording. The decision is whether those workflows, export options, and plan limits fit your meetings.

This is a source-based review of Otter's published product and help documentation, checked October 20, 2026. It is not a controlled accuracy benchmark or a new hands-on test. We build Anarlog, a competing meeting notetaker; the comparison below separates documented capabilities from choices you should test yourself.

## How does Otter AI work?

Choose a recording method before evaluating the summary. A visible meeting assistant and direct device recording have different setup and consent requirements, even when both produce an Otter conversation.

| Recording path | How capture starts | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Otter Notetaker | Joins a supported Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams meeting | Calendar auto-join, host admission, participant notice, and sharing settings |
| Desktop app | Captures a conversation from your computer without a meeting bot | Supported OS, audio permissions, selected audio sources, and upload completion |
| Mobile app or manual recording | You start the recording on your device | Microphone placement, network interruptions, and the final uploaded recording |

Otter documents [bot-free desktop recording for Mac and Windows](https://otter.ai/integrations/desktop). Choosing it changes how audio is captured; it does not establish on-device transcription or a local-only meeting archive. Otter's [processing guide](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/360048322493-Transcription-processing-time-FAQ) describes uploaded recordings being processed on its servers.

For the Notetaker route, review [auto-join controls](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/12906714508823-Stop-Otter-Notetaker-from-automatically-joining-your-meetings). Selecting **Meetings I manually select** changes the default, but previously toggled individual events retain their settings. Check upcoming events as well as the default. Tell participants when recording or transcription is active, whichever capture path you use.

## Can Otter record offline?

Otter's [offline-recording guide](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/44008744581271-Record-with-Otter-offline-or-without-an-internet-connection) says audio capture can continue when connectivity drops, while live transcription pauses. After reconnection, the app resumes transcription or uploads the completed recording for processing.

Keep the desktop app, mobile app, or recording browser tab open until uploading finishes. Otter warns that closing it early can lose audio or conversation data. This is temporary offline capture followed by online processing, not an offline transcription-and-summary workflow. If the latter is a requirement, compare the separate speech and summary stages in our [offline meeting transcription guide](/blog/offline-meeting-transcription-mac/).

## What languages does Otter support?

Otter documents transcription in [English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and Chinese (Simplified)](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/26660468516631-Transcribe-conversations-in-English-Spanish-French-German-Japanese-or-Chinese-Simplified). Check the language you need before purchasing. For mixed-language meetings, test the actual combination rather than treating support for individual languages as proof of reliable switching throughout a call.

## Which Otter features matter for a meeting workflow?

| Need | Documented capability | Decision to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Follow a meeting live | Live draft transcription, followed by post-meeting processing | Whether the recording contains both sides of your call and the terms you use |
| Produce a repeatable recap | Meeting templates with configurable summary sections | Whether the selected plan's template allowance covers your use |
| Ask about previous meetings | AI Chat can use conversations, channels, folders, and supported connected context | Which experience has reached your account and whether the cited source supports the answer |
| Reuse notes elsewhere | Transcript/audio exports and a separate Google Drive integration | Format, included content, plan eligibility, and workspace export restrictions |

[Custom Meeting Type Templates](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/31402572907415-Custom-Meeting-Type-Templates) let you configure summary sections and apply templates to recurring meeting types. Availability and usage allowances vary by plan.

Otter's [AI Chat Overview](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/19682180167575-Otter-AI-Chat-Overview), updated October 14, describes questions across conversations and a gradually rolling-out Chat/Work experience. Work can act through connected apps, so check its context and permissions before using it. Review the source references in answers: access to several meetings does not guarantee a correct cross-meeting conclusion.

For a practical trial, record a short, consented sample containing a name, a number, a rejected proposal, and an action item without an owner. Check the transcript, speaker assignments, and summary separately. Correct those details before sharing the result.

## Is Otter AI safe?

Evaluate recording notice, sharing, retention, model training, and your organization's agreement separately. A bot-free recorder can still use cloud processing, and a security feature does not answer who receives a particular transcript.

### What the August 2026 court order establishes

The court's [August 13, 2026 order in In re Otter.AI Privacy Litigation](https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/us-dis-crt-n-d-cal/322025.html), reproduced on FindLaw, granted the motion to dismiss in part and denied it in part. The court accepted sufficiently pleaded allegations for several privacy claims to proceed, including the CIPA third-party-interceptor theory. That is a pleading-stage decision, not a final finding that Otter violated the law. We verified this order, not a complete subsequent docket history.

### Data use and enterprise agreements

Otter's [privacy policy](https://otter.ai/privacy-policy), effective June 16, 2026, describes training its proprietary AI on de-identified audio and on transcriptions that may contain personal information. It also distinguishes processing under Business or enterprise service agreements, where the customer directs processing. Check the agreement and controls applicable to your workspace rather than assuming every plan has identical terms.

The policy also describes disclosures of device, cookie, and location information that may be considered a sale or targeted-advertising processing under some state privacy laws. That is different from a claim that Otter sells meeting recordings. For a sensitive meeting, establish the allowed data path, participants' permissions, sharing audience, and retention before recording. ## How much does Otter AI cost?

![Otter.ai's Basic, Pro, Business, and Enterprise pricing tiers on the monthly billing view](anarlog/blog/articles/otter-ai-review/otter-pricing-2026-09.png)

*Pricing screenshot captured in September 2026. The current allowances below were checked against [Otter's pricing page](https://otter.ai/pricing) on October 20; promotions, currency, and plan terms can change.*

| Plan | Listed USD price per user | Recording and import limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | Free | 300 monthly minutes; 30 minutes per conversation; three lifetime file imports |
| Pro | $16.99 monthly or $8.33/month billed annually | 1,200 monthly minutes; 90 minutes per conversation; ten monthly file imports |
| Business | $30 monthly or $19.99/month billed annually | Unlimited in-app recordings; four hours per conversation; 6,000 imported-file transcription minutes monthly |
| Enterprise | Custom quote | Confirm contracted allowances, controls, and add-ons |

Business lists unlimited file imports, but import count and imported-file transcription minutes are different limits. Do not read unlimited in-app recordings as unlimited processing of uploaded files. Basic's conversation history is limited to the 25 most recent conversations.

For ten Business seats, the listed base rates are $300 per month with monthly billing or $2,398.80 for a year with annual billing, before tax or promotions. Confirm current checkout terms rather than extrapolating a temporary discount.

## Can you export Otter notes and recordings?

Yes. Otter's [standard export guide](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/360047733634-Export-conversations) documents TXT for Basic and additional paid formats including DOCX, PDF, and SRT. Audio export uses MP3. Export options include speaker names, timestamps, highlights, and images; options depend on format and plan. Workspace controls may also restrict exports.

[Bulk export](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/13829342669079-Bulk-export-conversations-audio-or-Takeaways) is available on Business and higher plans. Before changing services, export a sample with the content you need and open it in your destination tool. Check timestamps, speaker names, attachments, and any summary you intend to keep; a transcript export is not automatically a complete workspace backup.

The [Google Drive integration](https://help.otter.ai/hc/en-us/articles/42385276728727-Export-to-Google-Drive) can export a conversation as a Google Doc or TXT file with selected content. It requires connecting Drive, and a workspace can hide the integration or disable exports. Automatic exports require Enterprise enablement through the account manager. Edits do not continuously sync: update the exported file manually after changing the conversation. Bulk exports to Drive are not supported. On mobile, saving through the device's share menu is a separate workflow from this integration.

## Is Otter AI worth it for your team?

Otter is a reasonable candidate when you want hosted meeting search and collaboration, its supported languages cover your calls, and its plans fit the recording and import workload. It is less suitable when your requirement is a complete offline AI workflow or when your organization cannot approve its data processing and sharing terms.

Start with a controlled sample. Confirm capture, review the transcript and recap, test an export, and check the plan's allowances. Use those results to choose the capture method, processing model, and plan that meet your requirements.
