Bot-free meeting assistants capture audio or meeting text through your device or browser without adding a recording participant to the call. That can suit calls where a meeting bot is unwelcome, but it does not establish how the content is processed or whether recording has been approved.

Compare the capture method, transcription and summary providers, stored data, and sharing settings separately. A bot-free tool can still send audio or text to a cloud service.

**Sources checked October 14, 2026.** This compares published capture paths and conditions, not a new hands-on benchmark of all twelve services. Meeting.ai's documented bot-free cases are in-person capture and uploads; its video-call workflow uses a bot.

## How to Choose a Bot-Free Workflow

Start with the call you need to capture: a browser meeting, native desktop client, phone conversation, or in-person discussion. Test that exact path before adopting a tool. An available mobile app, file upload, or calendar bot is not proof that the desktop video-call path is bot-free.

Then check the processing and archive separately. Anarlog offers independent transcription and summary selections; Notta distinguishes Privacy Mode from other workflows; Jamie documents server transcription; Superpowered distinguishes live audio deletion from temporary transcript retention. These are different configurations, not a universal privacy ranking.

Before enabling automatic capture or sharing, confirm the required participant notice and permission, inspect the recipients, and check whether you can correct and export the output. Verify monthly allowances, per-meeting limits, retention, and the price for your chosen billing period on the provider's current plan.

## Frequently asked questions

### 1. What are bot-free AI meeting assistants?

Bot-free tools capture audio or meeting text through your device or browser instead of adding a visible recording participant. Their processing, storage, and sharing settings still need a separate review.

### 2. How are bot-free meeting assistants different from bot-based assistants?

Compare recording methods rather than treating one as an automatic privacy or compliance guarantee. Some products offer both methods.

| Aspect | Bot-based capture | Bot-free capture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meeting presence | Adds a recording participant | Does not add a recording participant |
| Capture path | Recording participant receives meeting content | Device audio or browser meeting text, depending on the tool |
| AI processing | Depends on the provider and configuration | Depends on the provider and configuration |
| Notice and permission | Must be handled for the meeting | Must be handled for the meeting |
| Retention and sharing | Check the configured policy and recipients | Check the configured policy and recipients |

### 3. How do you take AI meeting notes without bots?

Choose a tool that supports the meeting platform and capture method you need. For audio capture, grant the required microphone and system-audio permissions. Then select the transcription and summary setup: [local AI meeting notes](/blog/local-ai-meeting-notes/) and cloud AI have different configuration and data paths. Check the result before sharing it.

### 4. How do botless meeting assistants work?

The capture path is specific to each tool. A desktop audio recorder, browser extension, and uploaded recording have different permissions and availability. Confirm whether your exact meeting client is supported rather than assuming bot-free capture works with every app.

### 5. How does consent work with bot-free meeting assistants?

Do not infer agreement from the absence of a bot or from a recording notice. Anarlog's [terms](/terms/) make the person recording responsible for required notice and consent. Confirm the workflow your organization requires before recording, and check the tool's disclosure, participant-response, and sharing controls. Audio deletion or local storage does not establish that participants approved capture.

### 6. Why would someone choose a bot-free meeting assistant over a bot-based one?

People often choose bot-free assistants because:

- They want to avoid awkward or distracting bot participants in sensitive meetings.
- They want to choose a capture method that fits their meeting platform and workflow.
- They need to assess processing, retention, and access controls independently of capture.
- They prefer seamless, natural conversations without extra virtual guests.
- They need a local setup and have verified both transcription and summary models work offline.
- They want full control over where and how meeting data is processed and stored.
